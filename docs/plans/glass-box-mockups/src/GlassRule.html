Why rest this long after heavy sets?

%%GRADE moderate%%Several reviews agree; details vary by study
What the app does

Rests 3:00 after T1 sets and 2:00 after T2 sets, because heavy sets need two minutes or more to keep strength gains coming. You can add time or skip at any point.

Where it could be wrong

Rest needs vary. If you feel ready sooner, going early is fine; after a very hard set, take longer.
